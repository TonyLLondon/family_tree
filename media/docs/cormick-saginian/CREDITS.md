# Cormick–Saginian family photographs

High-resolution scans of nineteenth-century photographs of **Dr William Cormick**
(royal physician in Tabriz, husband of Tamar Saginian) and **Tamar Saginian**
(youngest sister of [Anna Saginian](../../../people/anna-saginian.md)), with one
family group portrait and the firman issued to Cormick.

## Source

All images reproduced from the **Connections BMC** family-research blog post
*"Dr. Cormick: The man who met the Báb"* (Burgess–Maclean–Cormick family),
<https://connectionsbmc.wordpress.com/2013/01/14/134/> (gallery images
`manmet1`–`manmet7`). The blog is already catalogued in the vault as
[connectionsbmc-saginian-interview](../../../sources/connectionsbmc-saginian-interview.md);
these files extract the gallery at full resolution (the vault previously held
only a 38 KB thumbnail and the images embedded in a PDF).

The fez portrait was traced by researcher **Vincent Flannery** and first
published in *The Old Kilkenny Review* (1996). The original photographs date to
the 1860s–1870s and are **public domain by age**; the digital scans are courtesy
of the Connections BMC family blog.

## Note on William Cormick's burial

The same blog captions two further images as Cormick's gravestone at **Kensal
Green, London** (not reproduced here). This conflicts with the *Iran* journal
burial survey (Wright, 1998), which records "Dr. William Cormick died at Tabriz
on 30.12.1877 … and not in Shepherd's Bush," and with Encyclopaedia Iranica,
which places his grave in the Tabriz Armenian cemetery beside his father. The
discrepancy is unresolved and recorded here rather than reconciled.
