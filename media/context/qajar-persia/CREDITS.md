# Qajar-era Persia — context images

Engravings and plates from three illustrated travel books of the Qajar period
(c. 1860s–1890s), harvested from the Internet Archive and cropped to the plate.
All three works were published before 1900 and are in the **public domain**.

These are **context images** — period scenes of the court, processions, costume,
streets, caravanserais and architecture of the Tabriz → Tehran corridor and
beyond. They illustrate the world the family's Persian (Saginian / Daoud Khan)
line moved through; none depict a named relative.

## Provenance & method

Each plate was located inside the source PDF (downloaded from the Internet
Archive), rendered at high resolution, and cropped to the engraving — removing
page margins, the book gutter, and the binding strip. Landscape plates printed
sideways were rotated upright. No tonal content was altered beyond cropping and
rotation (the Curzon DLI plates are reproduced as-scanned).
