## Institutional context (useful for narrative)

### Founding and campuses

- **100 Jahre Gstaad Palace** (`100jahregstaadpa0000unse`, p. 208 OCR): *Institut Le Rosey* founded **1880** at **Rolle** on Lake Geneva; linked to Saanenland winter presence from **1917** (Gstaad term). Bibliography cites *Le Rosey: Un siècle de souvenirs* (Rolle, 1980) — **not on IA**.
- **I Survived Swiss Boarding Schools** (`isurvivedswissbo0000rich`, 2006, p. 210): student memoir; Le Rosey moves **Rolle ↔ Gstaad**; dining-hall discipline (teacher **Beauverre**); no Stump.
