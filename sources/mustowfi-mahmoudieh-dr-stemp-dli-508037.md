# Mustowfi — Mahmoudieh, ill-omened Mohammadieh, and Dr. Stemp (p. 51)

**Primary source:** Abdollah Mustowfi, *Sharh-e Zendegani-ye Man*, vol. 1, **p. 51** — Mohammad Shah builds **Mohammadieh** between Tajrish and Evin; the house is **ill-omened**; **Mahmoud Alamir** renames it **Mahmoudieh**; lands **belong to Dr. Stemp the dentist** at time of writing.
