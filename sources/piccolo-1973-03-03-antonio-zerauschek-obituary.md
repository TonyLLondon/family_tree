# Il Piccolo — Antonio Zerauschek death notice (3 March 1973)

Third published obituary for [Antonio Zerauschek](../people/antonio-zerauschek.md), in *Il Piccolo di Trieste* (Saturday **3 March 1973**, page 13). Dated **Firenze, 28 February 1973**. Names children Giuliana, Fulvia, Guido, Mario; brother Riccardo and wife Tatiana.
