# Death notice — Riccardo Zerauschek

> Trieste, 14 February 1978 — After a brief illness **Riccardo Zerauschek** passed away peacefully. The sorrowful announcement is given by his wife **TATIANA**, his daughter **NORA** and her husband **MARIO STARC**, his son **RENATO** and wife **SILVIA**, his son **LUCIANO** and wife **ALICE**, beloved grandchildren **ALESSANDRA, ROBERTA, MAXI, PIETRO, GIULIA and PAOLA**, and all relatives.
>
> Heartfelt thanks to Dr **NICOLO RELJA** for his affectionate care and to Sister **BONAVENTURA** for her generous assistance.
>
> The funeral will take place today, 14 February, at 10:30 from the chapel of the Maggiore Hospital.
