### Chronology: **wabā** season vs Edward’s **fever** (1853)

| When | Source | What it shows |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Aug 1853** | Issue **131** domestic news | **ناخوشی**; many fled Tehran for fear — same epidemic season as **وبا** in the issue **156** text |
| **30 Oct 1853** | [London *Evening Standard* dispatch (Tehran)](../1853-london-evening-standard-persia-burgess-gazette/transcription.md) | **Mr. Burgess** **confined to bed by fever**; substitute editor published a bad article |
| **≈ Jan 1854** | Issue **156** (this notice) | **Retrospective** royal **khilʿat** for staying in the capital during **ناخوشی و وبا** while others went to **یلاقات** and keeping the gazette running — **same 1853–early 1854 crisis**, **not** a separate wabā year |
| **Same issue, p. 2** | Marker band [`p176-200.fa.md`](transcription.marker/p176-200.fa.md) (pdf ~194) | Court print orders timed ahead of **عید نوروز** (~54 days before **Nowruz 1233 SH** = 21 March 1854) — consistent with **late January** publication |

**Takeaway:** The English press **fever** (Oct 1853) and the Persian court **wabā** reward (Jan 1854) are **sequential facets of one difficult season** — personal illness while the capital was emptied by sickness fear — not evidence of two unrelated epidemics.

## Persian (transcription)

چون خدمات عالیجاه **برجیس صاحب** **مباشر روزنامه** و **مترجم دیوان** در نظر اولیای دولت علیه **مقبول و مستحسن** افتاده، **خاصه** در اوقات **ناخوشی و وبا** که اغلب مردم **فرار کرده** به **یلاقات** رفته بودند، **مشار‌الیه** **محض پیشرفت** **خدمت محوله** **بخود** در **دارالخلافه مبارک** **مانده** و **کار روزنامه** را **به‌پرده نگذاشت** [برقرار نگه داشت] که **امور** روزنامه **متفرق** نشوند، **لهذا** در این اوقات از جانب **جناب ملوکانه** یک **طاقه شال ترمه کشمیری** **برسم خلعت** به عالیجاه مشار‌الیه **مرحمت** شد.

## English translation

Since the services of His Excellency **Burgess Ṣāḥeb**, **overseer (*mubāshir*) of the gazette** and **translator of the court (*motarjem-e dīvān*)**, have been found **acceptable and commendable** in the eyes of the ministers of the Exalted Government — **especially** in a time of **sickness and plague (*wabā*)**, when most people had **fled** to the **summer quarters (*yīlāqāt*)** — **he**, the aforesaid, **remained** in the **blessed capital (*Dār al-Khilāfah*)** **solely to advance the duties entrusted to him** and **did not let the work of the gazette lapse**, so that **the affairs of the newspaper should not fall apart**; **therefore**, at this time, from the **royal side**, **a roll of Kashmiri cashmere shawl** was **bestowed** upon the aforesaid Excellency **as a robe of honour (*khilʿat*)**.

## Notes

| Reading | Comment |
|---------|---------|
| **مباشر روزنامه** | Matches Kianfar / NLIR preface **مترجم** pairing; here both **mubāshir** and **motarjem-e dīvān** — stronger than “translator only.” |
| **وبا** | **1853–early 1854** epidemic season in Tehran (see **Issue and calendar dates** above); court reward published **issue 156 ≈ 26 Jan 1854**, after the **Oct 1853** fever dispatch — same season, not a new wabā year. |
| **یلاقات** | Summer retreats (Niāvarān etc.), not “Niāvarān” by name on this line. |
| **به‌پرده نگذاشت** | Lithograph read with PNG; sense = **did not abandon / kept the paper going** (Marker: `براه برده`). |
| **خلعت** | Standard Qajar court reward; **Kashmiri shawl** is the item named. |

### Secondary chronicle (same season)

Etemad al-Saltanah’s *Montaẓam-e Nāṣerī* (vol. 3 **p. 1756**) and *Morāʾat al-Bildān* (vol. 2 **p. 1179**) — indexed on [Ahlulbayt Library](https://ablibrary.net/) — list **برجیس صاحب، مباشر روزنامه و مترجم دیوان** with **میرزا عبد‌الله روزنامه‌نویس** among a **broader Nowruz-era خلعت همایون** batch (ranks, cloaks, badges). They **confirm titles and pairing** with the gazette scribe but **do not name the Kashmiri shawl** in the search excerpt.
