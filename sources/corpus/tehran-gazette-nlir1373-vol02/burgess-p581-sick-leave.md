**Takeaway:** This is the **formal gazette publication** Edward waited for in **February 1855** — his **first authorized return to England** since he reached Tabriz in **1831**. The notice reads as **routine medical leave**; Anna’s interview describes a **later, slower litter journey** toward Tabriz and death **~12 June 1855** before he reached home.

## Persian (transcription)

چون **عالیجاه برجیس صاحب** **مترجم این دولت عالیه** … **مباشر این روزنامه** بود، چون مدتی **مریض و علیل** بود و **اطباء معتمد** او را **در تغییر آب و هوا** دانسته بودند، و **پدر و اقربای** او نیز **از جانبش** خواهش کرده بودند که **اجازهٔ مریض** از این دولت عالیه گرفته **به ولایت انگلیس** رود **لندن** — در این اوقات **اذن مریض** از **امنای دولت عالیه** **بحضور شاهانه** حاصل نموده، **روانهٔ ولایت انگلیس** **خواهد شد**.

*(Adjacent items on the same lithograph page: French **hakīm** appointment; **Aghā Muʿīn** head valet — not transcribed here.)*

## English translation

Since His Excellency **Burgess Ṣāḥeb**, **translator of this Exalted Government** and **manager (*mubāshir*) of this newspaper**, had for some time been **ill and in poor health**, and the **physicians in whom he placed trust** judged that he needed a **change of air and climate**, and **his father and his relatives** had likewise **petitioned on his behalf** that **sick leave** be granted him by this Exalted Government so that he might go to the **country of England**, to **London** — **at this time**, by **royal command**, **sick leave** has been **obtained from the ministers of the Exalted Government**, and **he is to be sent** to the **country of England**.

## Notes

| Reading | Comment |
|---------|---------|
| **مباشر این روزنامه** | Same pairing as issue **156** (**مباشر روزنامه** + **مترجم دیوان**); here **مترجم این دولت عالیه**. |
| **اذن مریض** | Formal **medical leave** — matches Edward’s **Feb 1855** anxiety about **gazette** publication, not merely verbal PM assent. |
| **First leave to England since 1831** | Letters: decades **unable to leave Persia** (Charles’s debts, consular duty, gazette); this notice is state permission to **go home**, not the later emergency litter run Anna describes. |
| **Journey outcome** | Not in this notice — see [Edward Burgess](../../../people/edward-burgess.md) (Anna: Tabriz, **~12 Jun 1855**). |
