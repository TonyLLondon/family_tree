## Summary

**2nd Battalion, 1st (Royal) Regiment of Foot (Royal Scots)** at **Jubbulpore**, Central Provinces: arrived **1 March 1877**; ordered home **January 1879** and **January 1880** but **detained in India** by Viceroy telegrams owing to the **Second Anglo-Afghan War**; left Jubbulpore **1 October 1880**. The battalion did **not** deploy to the Afghan frontier.

Relevant to [William O'Byrne White](../../../people/william-obyrne-white.md) (regimental medical officer; [Gerald](../../../people/gerald-sebastian-white.md) born Jubbulpore **12 February 1878**).
