# Regimental Records of the Royal Scots — 2nd Bn, Jubbulpore & Afghan War detention (1877–1880)

**Source:** *The Regimental Records of the Royal Scots (The First or the Royal Regiment of Foot)* [1590–1913]. Internet Archive identifier `recordsroyalscots`. Compiled regimental history (open access).

## Transcription — 2nd Battalion (printed pp. 494–496; PDF pp. 580–582)

### Arrival at Jubbulpore (1877)

> The whole battalion marched from Agra en route for Saugor and Jubbulpore, on the 18th January, arriving on the 17th February, at Saugor, where a detachment of 3 companies … remained, the headquarters and 5 companies proceeding to Jubbulpore, where they arrived on the **1st March 1877**.

[Page image](../../media/docs/internet-archive/obyrne-white/records-royal-scots-2nd-bn-jubbulpore-afghan-detention-p580.png)

### 1878 — cholera at Saugor detachment

> On the 18th March, A, G, and H companies … proceeded to Saugor … Unfortunately, the detachment at Saugor was attacked with **cholera on the 20th August, 1878** … During the epidemic there were in all **13 seizures, and 9 deaths**.

### Detention owing to Afghan War (1878–1879)

> The battalion was under orders for England, and was to have sailed from Bombay, on the troopship **Jumna**, on the **24th January, 1879**, but a copy of a telegram from the Viceroy to the Secretary of State for India, was received on the **21st October, 1878**, intimating that, it was deemed expedient, owing to the **war with Afghanistan**, to **detain "The Royal Scots" in India**, until further orders.

[Page image](../../media/docs/internet-archive/obyrne-white/records-royal-scots-2nd-bn-jubbulpore-afghan-detention-p581.png)

### Second detention (1879–1880)

> The battalion was again under orders to proceed to England, and was to have embarked at Bombay, in the troopship **Euphrates**, on the **31st January, 1880**, but was again detained in India owing to the state of affairs in **Afghanistan**. The following telegram was received on the **26th December, 1879**, detaining the regiment :—
>
> **"Government has decided to retain 2nd Battalion 1st Foot in India for another year."**

### Departure (1880)

> The regiment left **Jubbulpore**, Central Provinces, on the **1st October, 1880**, for Deolali … embarked for home … at Bombay … on the **5th November**, and landed at Queenstown, on the **4th December**.

## Notes

- **William O'Byrne White** was the **2nd Bn/1st Foot** regimental surgeon ([London Gazette, 15 Mar 1870](../../london-gazette-1870-white-1st-foot/reference.md)); promoted **Surgeon-Major** **31 March 1877** ([Monthly Army List, May 1877](../1877-monthly-army-list-obyrne-white-surgeon-major/transcription.md)) — twelve days before the battalion reached Jubbulpore.
- Son **Gerald Sebastian White** was born at Jubbulpore **12 February 1878** during this garrison posting (*Homeward Mail*, 11 Mar 1878).
- The **1st Battalion** left for **Malta** in January 1878; only the **2nd Battalion** remained in India and was **retained as Central Provinces garrison** — not sent to Ali Musjid, Peiwar Kotal, or the Kabul columns. Stewart's *War Medals and Their History* (IA `warmedalstheirhi00stewrich`) lists regiments at each frontier action; **2nd Bn/1st Foot does not appear**.
- **Campaign medals:** No **Afghanistan 1878–80** medal is expected for William O'Byrne White on this evidence; the regiment's war role was **detention in India**, not frontier service. Any future medal search should target **named medal rolls** only if independent detachment is found (none located on Internet Archive full-text search).
