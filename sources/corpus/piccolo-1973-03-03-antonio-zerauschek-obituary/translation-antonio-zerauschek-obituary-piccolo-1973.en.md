# *Il Piccolo* — Antonio Zerauschek

**Newspaper:** *Il Piccolo* (Trieste)  
**Issue date:** Saturday 3 March 1973  
**Page:** 13 (death notices)

---

†

He passed away peacefully, far from his Zara.

**Antonio Zerauschek**

After burial, his children **Giuliana**, **Fulvia**, **Guido**, and **Mario** give the sad announcement, together with their sons-in-law, daughters-in-law, and grandchildren; his brother **Riccardo** with his wife **Tatiana** and their children; and all relatives.

Florence, 28 February 1973
