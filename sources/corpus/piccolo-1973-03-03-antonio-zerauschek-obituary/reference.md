# Il Piccolo — Antonio Zerauschek death notice (3 March 1973)

## Summary

Death notice in *Il Piccolo di Trieste*, **Saturday 3 March 1973**, page **13**. Dated **Firenze, 28 February 1973** — the civil death date already recorded on the person page and funeral card. Third published obituary alongside *Difesa Adriatica* and *ZARA* (A.N.D.A.Z.).

**Key facts:** died peacefully far from Zara; surviving children Giuliana, Fulvia, Guido, Mario; brother Riccardo with wife Tatiana.
