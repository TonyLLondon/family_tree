## Section header and origin

**1713. Иван Барамович (1787–?)**

**КНЯЗЬЯ САГИНОВЫ (САГИНАШВИЛИ)** — **САГИНОВЫ (САГИНАШВИЛИ)**, also **Kaplanovich (Glakhovich)**, **они же САГИДЗЕ** — Kakhetian princely house. Catherine II raised them; Senate resolution **23 September 1859**; origin traced to the **Dadiani** princes of Mingrelia (per letters of 1771 to General Totleben and King Solomon I). Listed among Kakhetian princes in the appendix to the **Treaty of Georgievsk (1783)**. Enrolled in **Part V** of the Tiflis guberniya genealogical book.

No arms confirmed in Russia; V. Tsikhinsky assigns the same arms as the Dadiani (tradition of common origin). Genealogy in **table 112**. A separate untitled **Saginov (Saginashvili)** line also exists.

## Named representatives (prose list)

- **Lieutenant-General Alexander Dmitrievich Saginov (1808–1887)** — enrolled in Part I of the genealogical book (Highest confirmation **December 1850** and **24 June 1856**). Graduated **Tiflis Noble School**; career officer, lieutenant-general from 1860, deputy commander of troops in Tiflis, Elisavetpol, Baku, and Erivan guberniyas; Knight of many orders; married Princess Varvara Smaragdova Dzhambakurian-Orbeliani, then Princess Elizaveta Chavchavadze (sister of Ilia Chavchavadze); no children, adopted nephew **Ivan Vissarionovich Saginov**.

- **Mamuka Givievich** — wife Darija, daughters Nina, Efemia, Tasia (№ 24–26).

- **Androz (Andaros) Givievich** — wife Anna; sons **Georgii and David (№ 15, 27–28)**.

- **Psata Zurabovich** — wife Marfa; sons Eleazar, Iosif (Isse), Mikhail (№ 13, 21–23).

- **Luarsab Georgievich (№ 29)**.

- **Widow of Garsevan, Ketevana**, and **their son Zaal (№ 31)**.

- **David Sabovich** — wife Efimia; daughter Anna (№ 19, 35).

- **Nikolai Alexandrovich** and sisters Tamara, Natalia (№ 32–35).

## Vault reading

- Confirms a documented **princely Saginov / Saginashvili** house with **David** and **Zaal** as recurring given names in **table 112**.
- **Does not** name parents of **Daoud Khan Saginian** or tie **1811** deserters to a numbered row in this excerpt.
- **Alexander D. Saginov (1808–1887)** is the same documented officer generation referenced in [surname-saginian.md](../../../topics/surname-saginian.md) as **circumstantial** context for Tiflis noble military education — not proof of shared father **Dmitry** with Daoud (~1790).
