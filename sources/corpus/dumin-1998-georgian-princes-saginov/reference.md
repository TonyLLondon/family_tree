# Dumin & Chikovani (1998) — Князья Сагиновы (Сагинашvili)

## Summary

Excerpt from **S. V. Dumin & Yu. K. Chikovani**, *Dvorianskie rody Rossiiskoi imperii. Tom 4: Kniaz'ia Tsarstva Gruzinskogo* (Moscow, 1998), **printed pp. 197–200** in the volume (repo PDF **pages 202–205** of the Internet Archive text-layer file). Documents the princely house **Сагиновы (Сагинашvili)**, also **Kaplanovich / Сагидзе**, Kakhetian origin, Georgievsk Treaty list (1783), genealogical **table 112**, and nineteenth-century officers including **Alexander Dmitrievich Saginov (1808–1887)** (Tiflis Noble School, lieutenant-general).

**Genealogical limit for this vault:** the entry names many **Davids** and a **Заал (№ 31)** in table prose, but **does not** identify **Daoud Khan Saginian (~1790, fled 1811)** or his brother **Zaal Saginean** with parents. Link to the Qajar line remains **circumstantial** (same noble stratum and patronymic patterns).
