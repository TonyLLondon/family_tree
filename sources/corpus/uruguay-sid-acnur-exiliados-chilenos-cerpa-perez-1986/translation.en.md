# Uruguay SID — ACNUR Chilean exile roster — English notes

**Source:** Uruguayan military intelligence (*Servicio de Información de las FF.AA.*), filed **30 October 1986** (date stamp on cover).

## Report summary (p. 318)

> **Subject: Roster of Chilean exiles**  
> **Evaluation:** F-3  
> **Origin:** Agency 108  
> **Text:** UNHCR (**ACNUR**) has distributed a roster of Chilean citizens in exile.  
> **Annex:** No. 1 — Roster of Chilean exiles.

The Uruguayan SID copied or intercepted the **ACNUR (UN High Commissioner for Refugees)** master list of Chileans abroad — part of the post-dictatorship exile bureaucracy that tracked refugees after the 1973 coup. The document is **Uruguayan provenance** (Archivos del Terror / SID Berrutti archive) but the **name list itself is Chilean/UNHCR in origin**.

## Helia’s entry (p. 322)

> **CERPA PEREZ HELIA DEL CARMEN**

*(Annex is alphabetised; adjacent lines on the facsimile are not evidence of family relationship.)*

This is **independent** of:

| Source | What it proves |
|--------|----------------|
| [*Cartilla del Retornado* (1985)](../cartilla-retornado-prohibicion-ingreso-1985/translation.en.md) | Chilean **police ban** on re-entry |
| [Cambio21 / Corporación list (2018)](../cambio21-405-mujeres-estadio-nacional-2018/) | **Estadio Nacional** detention, Sept–Oct 1973 |
| [Exonerados transparency (2010)](../exonerados-politicos-transparencia-2010/) | **SRCEI** dismissal 16 Apr 1974 |

Here Helia appears on a **1986 ACNUR exile register** — consistent with still being counted among Chileans abroad more than a decade after the coup, while the *Cartilla* (July 1985) still barred her return.

## Research use

- Confirms **Helia del Carmen Cerpa Pérez** as a named **Chilean exile** in UNHCR circulation (1986).
- Suggests tracing **ACNUR / UNHCR Chile exile files** (if declassified) for case-level detail — embassy route, host country, family unit.
- **Not** a substitute for Italian-embassy rosters (Archivo Nacional caja 271) or UK JWG arrival records — different agencies, same exile period.
