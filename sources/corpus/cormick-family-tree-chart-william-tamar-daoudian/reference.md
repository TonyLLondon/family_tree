Printed horizontal tree titled **The CORMICK Family**; subtitle **Cormicks who lived in Persia shown in bold**. **Compiler, date, and publication** not visible on the scan in the vault.

## Why this matters for Tamar Saginian

- Chart gives **William** × **Tamar DAOUDIAN**, **m. 1850 Tabriz**, with **Tamar** born **c. 1830 Persia**, died **1899 London** — **same marriage** described elsewhere as **Dr. William Cormick** × **Tamar Saginian** / **Tamar Daoudian**.
- **DAOUDIAN** patronymic = “child of **Daoud**,” aligned with **Anna’s 1880 interview** (Tamar youngest **sister**, both **daughters of Daoud Khan**) — **not** with [Armen Saginian (2022)](../armen-saginian-2022-thank-you-america-gorgin-descendants-tamar-chapter/reference.md), which places **Tamara Khanum** as **Gorgin Khan**’s daughter.
- [Armen’s memoir](../armen-saginian-2022-thank-you-america-gorgin-descendants-tamar-chapter/transcription.md) uses **Dr. William McCormick**; this chart uses **Cormick** — treat as **one person** (spelling variance).

## Vitals to reconcile

- Chart: **William b. 1819 Tabriz**. Vault person page still carries **1822** with note of conflict — update when a single primary standard is chosen.
