# Gazzetta Ufficiale — Antonio Zerauschek, presidente E.P.T., Zara (7 April 1938)

## Summary

Official notice listing **Antonio Zerauschek, son of Antonio**, as **presidente E.P.T.** (Ente Provinciale del Turismo) for **Zara**. Confirms his public role in interwar Zadar tourism alongside his commercial and industrial profile.
