# Il Piccolo — A. Zerauschek Zara ads (26–28 October 1923)

## Summary

Three consecutive *Il Piccolo* issues carry **A. ZERAUSCHEK — ZARA** contact lines: **26 Oct** (horse breeding, Prestranek/Lipizza); **27 Oct** (existing corpus); **28 Oct** (fine liquors — Ananas, Cognac, Maraschino, etc.).
