# Armen Saginian (2022) — *Who Was My Father*

**Full work:** Armen Saginian, *Thank You, America & Americans* (Xlibris, 2022).  
**This bundle:** facsimile of **WHO WAS MY FATHER** — Hovsep Sarginian’s introduction, Solomon Khan / Nunya Ter-Ohanian, then **Treaty of Gulistan (24 Oct 1813)** and flight of **Heracles II** with **Colonel David Khan Saginashvilli**, **Captain Gorgin Khan**, **Captain Zali Khan**, and **Crown Prince Alexei**; assignments at Tehran, Isfahan (artillery school), Tabriz (Azerbaijan cavalry); **Mary, Mother of God** cemetery; spread **cuts off** mid-sentence (“I learned that high ranking”). **Page numbers** not visible on scan.

## Conflicts and dating (vault)

| Claim in this chapter | Other vault evidence |
|------------------------|----------------------|
| Emigration framed **after Treaty of Gulistan (1813)** with **David** as **colonel** and **father** to **Gorgin** and **Zali** | [Maeda 2019](../maeda-2019-enikolopians-saginian-flight/reference.md), Anna’s milieu, Yaghoubian: **David** and **Zal** fled **1811** as **brothers**; David is **Sartip / Brigadier** in Qajar service, not “two sons” with Gorgin |
| **Heracles II** / **Heracles II** / **Bagratouni** spellings | Standard English historiography uses **Heraclius II** and **Bagratid/Bagratuni**; retained in [transcription](./transcription.md) as printed |
| Narrative continuity with [Dawood Khan chapter](../armen-saginian-2022-thank-you-america-dawood-khan-chapter/transcription.md) | Same memoir uses **Dawood** for the patriarch in one chapter and **David** here — treated as same figure in family oral tradition; **do not merge** into JSON without primary record |
