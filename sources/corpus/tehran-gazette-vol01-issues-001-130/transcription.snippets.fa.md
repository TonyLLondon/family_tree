# Tehran Gazette — excerpt transcriptions (vol. 1 facsimile)

Agent transcription from high-DPI PNGs unless noted. Surya OCR on the **1373 preface** block below matches [p014.png](pages-png/p014.png) (PDF page index **14**, not the volume cover sheets).

## NLIR 1373 preface — named staff (PDF p. 14)

![Preface staff list](pages-png/p014.png)

Modern typeset introduction (not lithograph). On identifying the newspaper’s staff:

> دست اندرکاران روزنامه را به سادگی نمی‌توان شناسائی کرد. … بنابراین شناساندن مسئولان روزنامه از آغاز تا انجام نیازمند سالها مطالعه و تحقیق است. مشهورترین دست اندرکاران روزنامه عبارتند از:  
> ۱ ـ حاج میرزا جبار تذکره‌چی (پدر میرزا جوادخان سعدالدوله) مدیر روزنامه.  
> **۲ ـ برجیس صاحب، مترجم روزنامه.**  
> ۳ ـ میرزا عبدالله، نویسنده فارسی روزنامه.

Notes that fuller biographies of Mirza Jabbar and **Berjīs Ṣāḥeb** appear in Taqizadeh’s press-history article (*Kāveh*, new series, year 2, no. 6) and briefly in Bamdad’s *Rajal-e Iran*.

## Issue 1 — masthead and opening (PDF p. 17)

![Issue 1 masthead](pages-png/p017.png)

**Title (masthead):** روزنامچه اخبار دارالخلافه طهران  
**Emblem line:** یا اسد الله الغالب (above lion-and-sun)

**Opening (paraphrase for readability; lithograph line breaks collapsed):**  
From the king’s heroic intent to educate subjects, nobles, loyal people, merchants, and tradesmen of the Exalted State — that they may gain knowledge and insight and be informed of inner and outer news — publication of this [paper] in the Guarded Domains was commanded, so that awareness and understanding among the people of this Exalted State may increase.

**Section header (visible):** اخبار خود دارالخلافه — news of the capital itself.

**Date on issue:** Friday **5** Rabīʿ al-Thānī **1267** (= **7 February 1851**).

## Foreign news — England (PDF p. 19, early 1851 run)

![Foreign news England](pages-png/p019.png)

**Section header:** اخبار دول خارجه — **انگلیس** (news of foreign states — England).

Agent summary of lithograph content (not line-by-line OCR): provincial **Iran** reports (Mazandaran, Isfahan, Gilan) occupy the upper column; the **England** block summarises **London** diplomatic and commercial news — **East India Company** shipping, **Bombay** and **Calcutta** sailings, and related **Hindustan** items. This is the column type Edward describes as ~**40 pages Persian per month** of European news for the King and prime minister ([letter, 22 Apr 1851](../../burgess-persian-letters-full-volume/transcription.md)).

## Edward’s voice (letters — use with this facsimile)

Not printed *in* the gazette, but **primary evidence** of what editing meant:

> *“I have the whole management of this newspaper on my shoulders … Meerza Hussein Kouli … began criticising a passage in one of the articles in the gazette, upon a question of style … ‘Why Burgess is vexed.’”* — Tehran, **22 April 1851**, to George.

> *“Hadji Abdul Mahomed — if the paper is not out before 12 o'clock tomorrow I shall complain to the Govt …”* — **16 June 1851**.

> *“I publish nothing except the foreign news, without the prime minister having previously revised it … One mistake … was the official announcement in the Gazette of the appointment of a general … before the appointment had taken place!”* — **22 September 1852** (after Amir Kabir).

## Note on the editor

These founding issues attribute the gazette to **royal command** and print **anonymous** official prose. **No name** for Edward Burgess (برجیس صاحب) appears on the transcribed mastheads or opening columns; the NLIR preface (PDF p. 14) names him as **مترجم روزنامه**.
