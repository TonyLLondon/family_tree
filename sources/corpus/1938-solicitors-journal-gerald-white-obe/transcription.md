## Notes

Legal-press corroboration of the same honour gazetted in the *London Gazette* (Supplement 34469, 1 January 1938). See [London Gazette scan](../../../media/docs/London-Gazette-34469-supplement-p14-White-OBE.png) and [Gerald Sebastian White](../../../people/gerald-sebastian-white.md).

The entry confirms full name **Gerald Sebastian White**, title **Registrar**, and court **High Court of Judicature at Fort St. George, Madras** — consistent with the Madras High Court directory and [1906 Madras Weekly Mail](../1906-madras-weekly-mail-gerald-white-new-solicitor/) admission notice.
