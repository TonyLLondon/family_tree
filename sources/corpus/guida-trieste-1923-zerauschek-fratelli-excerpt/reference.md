# Guida Trieste 1923 — Zerauschek F.lli, Zara (excerpt)

## Summary

Excerpt from the 1923 *Guida generale di Trieste* commercial directory. Lists **Zerauschek F.lli Giov. e Giusto** at **calle Catena**, Zara — the brothers' cigarette workshop before Antonio's later Manifattura Zaratina acquisition (1928).
