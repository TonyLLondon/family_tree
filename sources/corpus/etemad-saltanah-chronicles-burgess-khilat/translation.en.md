# English gloss — Etemad al-Saltanah Burgess passages

## *Montaẓam-e Nāṣerī* / *Morāʾat al-Bildān* (p. 1756 / 1179 cluster)

In the same extended court honours sequence:

- **Kārmak Ṣāḥeb**, chief physician of Azerbaijan, received a first-class **sarhangī** badge.
- **Burgess Ṣāḥeb**, **overseer of the gazette** and **translator of the court**, and **Mīrzā ʿAbd Allāh**, **writer of the gazette**, were **raised by an imperial khilʿat**.
- **Ḥājī Bābā**, son of the late merchant **Āqā Ḥusayn** of Baku, received a second-class **sarhangī** badge.
- (Many further grants of turban cloth, **termeh** cloaks, ranks, and horses follow in the same list.)

**Gloss:** Confirms Edward’s **dual title** and pairs him with **Mīrzā ʿAbd Allāh**; does **not** replace the gazette’s explicit **Kashmiri shawl** wording.

## *Majmaʿ al-Fuṣaḥā* (p. 1511)

Riza Quli Khan’s literary circle discusses **plague** and whether court **portraiture** should be attempted; **Burgess Ṣāḥeb** is named in the rhetorical frame. No grant, no likeness.
