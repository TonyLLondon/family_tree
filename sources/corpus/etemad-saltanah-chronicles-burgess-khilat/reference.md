# Etemad al-Saltanah — **برجیس صاحب** khilʿat & wabā (secondary chronicle)

**Author:** Muḥammad Ḥasan Khān **Etemad al-Saltanah** (d. 1313/1896).  
**Capture:** Full-text **search snippets** on [Ahlulbayt Digital Library (ablibrary.net)](https://ablibrary.net/search?query=%22%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%AC%DB%8C%D8%B3+%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A8%22), mirrored [`mirror.html`](mirror.html). The site indexes printed editions (e.g. *Tārīkh-e Montaẓam-e Nāṣerī*, *Morāʾat al-Bildān*, *Majmaʿ al-Fuṣaḥā*) but does not expose stable public PDF URLs for whole volumes in this workflow; passages below are transcribed from search hit text and checked against the same strings in the live reader UI.

## Why this bundle exists

| Primary | Secondary (this bundle) |
|---------|-------------------------|
| [*Vaqāye ʿ-e Ettefāqiye* issue **156** ≈ Jan 1854 — **one Kashmiri shawl** for staying in Tehran during **ناخوشی و وبا**](../tehran-gazette-nlir1373-vol02/burgess-p186-royal-reward.md) | Etemad’s court annals list **برجیس صاحب** among a **wider Nowruz-era khilʿat batch** (with **میرزا عبد‌الله** the gazette scribe) |
| Names Edward as **مباشر روزنامه** + **مترجم دیوان** | Same titles in *Montaẓam* / *Morāʾat* snippets |
| Single **طاقه شال ترمه کشمیری** | Chronicle lines say **خلعت همایون** without repeating the shawl item in the excerpt |

**Working conclusion:** The chronicle and the gazette notice describe **the same honour season** (1853–early 1854 epidemic / court calendar), not two unrelated rewards. The gazette gives the **item and rationale**; Etemad gives the **court-list context** (many recipients, turban cloth, cloaks, ranks).
