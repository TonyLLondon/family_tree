## Notes

Confirms the **first commission date of 31 March 1865** recorded in the Army Medical Department half-pay register and [William O'Byrne White](../../../people/william-obyrne-white.md) person page. The October 1865 issue lists him at **Aldershot** — consistent with Netley/Royal Victoria Hospital training and home-station posting before India.

Corroborates BNA/FMP newspaper trail (e.g. *Homeward Mail*, 12 December 1870 — appointment to 1st Foot) and [London Gazette, 15 March 1870](../london-gazette-1870-white-1st-foot/) regimental transfer.
