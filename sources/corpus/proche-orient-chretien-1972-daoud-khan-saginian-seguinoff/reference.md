# Proche-Orient chrétien 1972 — Daoud Khan Saginian / Sarparast (excerpt)

## Summary

Excerpt from *Proche Orient chrétien*, vol. 22 (1972), footnote **(125)** and adjacent prose on the **Sarparast** (Nestorian protector) appointment and successors. Confirms **Daoud (ou David) Khan Saginian (ou Séguinoff)** de Tabriz as first **Sarparast**, **Armenian from Georgia**, Persian army officer; adds **Anna (1815 – 8 Jan 1892)** × **Edward Burgess** (autumn 1851), son **John** (d. 16 Nov 1853, age 21 months), tombs at **Saint Thaddée-Barthélemy**, Tehran.

## Note on Tamar / Navasargian

Alice Navasargian’s *Immortals* (and the 2012 part-2 PDF already in vault) names **Tovmas Tovmasean** as Tamar Saginian’s husband in **running prose**, but **caption blocks on the same plates** identify **Dr. McCormic / Cormick** — see [Tamar Saginian](../../../people/tamar-saginian.md). Treat **Tovmasean** as Navasargian prose error or conflation, not a second husband.
