# Armen Saginian (2022) — Gorgin Khan, Elizabeth Khanum, **Tamara Khanum**, Sultan Khanum

**Full work:** Armen Saginian, *Thank You, America & Americans* (Xlibris, 2022).  
**This bundle:** two-page facsimile **continuing mid-sentence** (“officers were mostly…”) — **not** the start of a chapter in the vault. **Colonel Gorgin Khan Saginashlilli**, **Elizabeth Khanum** (Khosravan priest’s daughter), lists children **Solomon, Davud, Zali, Ivan** and **Tamara Khanum, Mayi Khanum, Sultan Khanum**; **Davud**’s descendants (in author’s wife’s line); **Tamara Khanum** married **Dr. William McCormick**, crown prince’s physician, **Tabriz Citadel**; **Sultan Khanum** biographical sketch; **Hovsep** as youngest son of **Slomon** [sic] Khan; **embedded portrait** with two captions — see below.

## Facsimile — photograph captions

The scan places **one** portrait between:

1. “HERE IS A PICTURE OF **SULTAN KHANUM**”  
2. “HERE IS A PICTURE OF **MY FATHER AS A TEENAGER**”

The visible subject reads **masculine / young man**. Reasonable reading: **layout or caption error** in the POD book — the image likely illustrates **Hovsep as teenager** (matches lower caption) rather than Sultan Khanum; **not resolved** here. Armen’s text separately claims a “picture of her in ladies' attire” for Sultan.
