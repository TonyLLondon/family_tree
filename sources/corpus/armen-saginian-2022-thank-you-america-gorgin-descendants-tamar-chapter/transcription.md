# Gorgin Khan descendants (incl. Tamara Khanum) — transcript

## Left page

officers were mostly from the nobility. The last dynasty ruling over Georgia was the Bagratuni Family. According to old traditions, the Bagratuni branches that ruled over Georgia and Armenia, have biblical heritage from King David's children. Some scholars reject the notion that they were of Jewish decent. However, some other scholars believe that Bagratunis were from Parthian ancestry. Regardless which claim is right or wrong, the fact remains that high-ranking officers from Georgia accompanied King Heracles II to Iran; a father General and a son Colonel remained and settled in Iran.

Colonel Gorgin Khan Saginashlilli was a rascal of a man. The only remaining photograph of him portrays his wild spirit. In one of his expeditions in central Azerbaijan, near Salmast, in a village called Khosravan, populated by Assyrians, he saw a girl on top of a ladder doing something on the wall or the roof. She was the daughter of the Catholic Priest of the village. He was the commander of the cavalry that was on an expedition after some troublemakers. He decided that he is going to marry that girl. And he did. She was Elizabeth Khanum. She became the matriarch of the Saginashvilis in Iran. She had several sons and several daughters by Gorgin Khan. The sons were: Solomon Khan, Davud Khan, Zali Khan, Ivan Khan, and the daughters were: Tamara Khanum, Mayi Khanum, Sultan Khanum, and one or two others that I do not have names for and information of.

Zali Khan had married and had a daughter called Satenik. He left Satenik with his brother Solomon and went to Kazakhstan, never to be heard from. Davud Khan married Anna Shahumian, and they had two sons and one daughter. The sons were Gurgen and Vincent. Gurgen married Ophelia Mahdesi Sarkissian, and they had one daughter named Astghik (Assik), who is my beloved wife and companion of my life. Vincent died as a playboy bachelor. Daughter (Elizabeth) married and had two daughters and one son. Ivan Khan remains unknown to me. I know that he had married a woman named Mariam, they had two daughters, but that is all that I know.

## Right page

Tamara Khanum married Dr. William McCormick, an English Physician, who was the doctor of the crown prince who was being trained for kingship in Tabriz Citadel.

Mayi Khanum married a Mr. Nercessian. Sultan Khanum was another rascal like her father. She should have been born a man. She was the commander of a fifty-men task-force who were carrying weapons and ammunition to Armenian fighters in Turkey, on their backs, through the mountains. She usually wore boots, had three bandoliers hanging on her chest, a Mosin rifle hang from her shoulder, and a Mauser handgun from her gun-belt. She used to smoke a long wooden pipe, (two feet long and over an inch in diameter), and never hesitated to beat the carriers with the wooden pipe. My dad used to say that he was scared to death of her. She was such a foulmouthed woman that men would not dare to challenge her authority. I have a picture of her in ladies' attire, but do not let her beauty deceive you, there is fire in her eyes.

My father, Hovsep Saginian (Joseph), was the youngest son of Slomon Khan. He was spoiled rotten. He was very limited in his mental faculties. He spoke in three languages of Armenian, Turkish and French, and he was literate in Armenian and French.
