## Summary

**Étienne Stump** mandamus against the Illinois State Board of Dental Examiners — full trade-press coverage in *The Dental Digest*, Vol. 8, No. 8 (August 1902). Court caption spells relator **Etienne Stump**; Board refused license **30 April 1902** (German-American Dental College diploma). Pairs with [Chicago Tribune / *Dental Register* reprint](../dental-register-stump-mandamus-1902/).
