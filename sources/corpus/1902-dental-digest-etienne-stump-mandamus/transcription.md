# The Dental Digest, August 1902 — Étienne Stump mandamus

**Source:** *The Dental Digest*, Vol. 8, No. 8 (published 15 August 1902). Internet Archive `sim_dental-digest_the-dental-digest_1902-08_8_8`.

## Editorial — “The Finale of One Bogus Diploma Mill” (printed p. 699)

> We publish in full in this issue the decision by Judge Chetlain in the case where a graduate of the German-American Dental College brought mandamus proceedings against the Illinois State Board of Dental Examiners to compel them to issue a license on his diploma. … Even the plaintiff, **Mr. Stump**, is satisfied with the justice of the ruling, as is shown by his letter, published in this issue of the Digest.

[Page image](../../media/docs/internet-archive/etienne-stump/dental-digest-1902-08-p71-stump.png)

## Court caption (printed p. 705)

> **DECISION AGAINST THE GERMAN-AMERICAN DENTAL COLLEGE BY JUDGE CHETLAIN.**
>
> STATE OF ILLINOIS, County of Cook. IN THE SUPERIOR COURT OF COOK COUNTY.
>
> **Propte Ex. Rel. ETIENNE STUMP** vs. **THE STATE BOARD OF DENTAL EXAMINERS.**
>
> This is a petition by the relator, **Etienne Stump**, for a writ of mandamus to compel the State Board of Dental Examiners to issue to him a license to practice dentistry. The relator claims that on the **30th day of April, 1902**, he then being in every way qualified to practice dentistry, applied to the Board for a license to practice in this state, exhibited a diploma from the **German-American Dental College**, and tendered to it the license fee of $5.00 …

[Page image](../../media/docs/internet-archive/etienne-stump/dental-digest-1902-08-p77-stump.png)

## Ruling excerpt (printed p. 710)

> On April 30, 1902, when relator applied for his license … The Board thereupon took action, the record of which is as follows:
>
> “The application of **Etienne Stump** for a license on a diploma from the German-American Dental College was renewed before the Board and **refused** for the reason that the Board was not satisfied with the reputability of the German-American Dental College.”

## Notes

- **Spelling:** Caption and Board record use **Etienne Stump** — matches parish Tallinn (**Étienne Marc Theodore Stump**, b. 7 March 1880).
- Same proceeding as [Chicago Tribune, 10 Aug 1902](../dental-register-stump-mandamus-1902/transcription.en.md) / [*Dental Register* reprint](../../dental-register-stump-mandamus-1902/extracted.pdf.md); this IA volume adds the **full Digest layout** and editorial framing.
- License refusal (**April 1902**) precedes the **~1904 New York hire** with **Atabak** and departure for Iran ([Beytoote translation](../beytoote-stump-dentist-atabak-ahmadshah/translation.en.md)).
