## Notes

- **ناخوش‌بوم / بدیمن:** ill-omened land — Mustowfi’s phrase for the estate’s reputation after Mohammad Shah’s death.
- **ناف‌بری:** idiomatic “fresh beginning” — Alamir’s renaming ritual, not merely a new building wing.
- **استمپ:** Mustowfi’s spelling; matches Mazda English translation “Stemp.”
- **Time of writing:** Mustowfi completed the memoir in the 1940s; “فعلاً” (at present / now) refers to his contemporary ownership, not Mohammad Shah’s era.
