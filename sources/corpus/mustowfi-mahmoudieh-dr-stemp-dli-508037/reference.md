# Mustowfi — Mahmoudieh / Mohammadieh / Dr. Stemp (vol. 1, p. 51)

**Author:** Abdollah Mustowfi (عبدالله مستوفی), *Sharh-e Zendegani-ye Man* (شرح زندگانی من), **vol. 1**.

**Facsimile:** printed **p. 51** in the Mustowfi volume — **PDF p. 72** (1-based) in the IA holder file `dli.ernet.508037`.

**Internet Archive:** [dli.ernet.508037](https://archive.org/details/dli.ernet.508037) — DLI scan titled *Tareekh Ijtimayee Wadaree Dora Kajaria Part 1*; the Mustowfi memoir text is embedded in this holder volume.

## What this passage establishes

| Claim | Mustowfi (p. 51) |
|-------|------------------|
| Mohammad Shah built **Mohammadieh** between **Bagh-e Ferdous (Tajrish)** and **Evin** | Yes |
| He **never lived there** | Yes |
| Estate was **ill-omened** — buyers fell into misfortune | Yes (*ناخوش‌بوم* / *بدیمن*) |
| **Mahmoud Alamir** (*Ehtesham al-Saltana*) renamed it **Mahmoudieh** | Yes |
| Lands **belong to Dr. Stemp the dentist** at time of writing | Yes — **دکتر استمپ دندانساز** |

**Not in this passage:** guesthouse, Vera Obolensky, 40 cabins, clinic on Lalezar.

## Spelling

Mustowfi writes **استمپ** (Stemp), not اشتومپ or اشتمپ. The English Mazda translation (Mostofi-Glenn, 1997) uses **"Dr. Stemp the dentist."**
