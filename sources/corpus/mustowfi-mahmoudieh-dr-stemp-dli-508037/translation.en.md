# Mustowfi — Mahmoudieh, the ill-omened palace, and Dr. Stemp

**Source:** Abdollah Mustowfi, *Sharh-e Zendegani-ye Man* (*An Account of My Life*), vol. 1, **p. 51** (printed).  
**English scholarly translation:** Abdollah Mostofi, *Administrative and Social History of the Qajar Period*, tr. Nayer Mostofi-Glenn (Mazda Publishers, 1997) — same passage under “Dr. Stemp the dentist.”  
**Facsimile:** [mustowfi-mahmoudieh-dr-stemp-p51-facsimile.png](../../../media/docs/mustowfi/mustowfi-mahmoudieh-dr-stemp-p51-facsimile.png)

## The passage (translated)

The page sits in Mustowfi’s chapter **“In the reign of Mohammad Shah.”** After describing Haji Mirza Aqasi’s canal works from the **Jajrud** and **Karaj** rivers toward Tehran, Mustowfi turns to the shah’s last years:

> Mohammad Shah was often unwell. In summer he would first stay at **Negaristan**, and when the weather turned cold he would move to **Ehsarabad**. Toward the end of his life he built **Mohammadieh** between **Bagh-e Ferdous (Tajrish)** and **Evin** — but **he never lived in it**. After him it passed through several hands, and **whoever bought it fell into some misfortune**; fate marked it as **ill-omened** (*badi-man* / *nakhosh-boom*). For that reason the **last owner, Ehtesham al-Saltana Mahmoud Alamir**, gave it a **fresh start** (*naf-buri* — literally “cutting the umbilical cord,” i.e. a new naming and wing) and renamed it **Mahmoudieh** — **which at present belongs to Dr. Stemp the dentist**.

---

## What a genealogist can rely on here

This is **primary Persian evidence** from a Qajar finance official who knew the capital’s elite households. It is not a newspaper synthesis or a family email. It confirms, in Mustowfi’s own voice:

1. **Mohammad Shah’s palace** was called **Mohammadieh** — built on the **Tajrish–Evin** slope (today’s Mahmoudieh district).
2. The shah **died without occupying** the house he had built.
3. The estate carried a **reputation for bad luck** — each purchaser suffered “some calamity” (*balā*).
4. **Mahmoud Alamir** (*Ehtesham al-Saltana*, 1863–1936) gave the tract a **fresh start** (*naf-buri*) and **renamed** it **Mahmoudieh** — the name the neighbourhood still bears.
5. By Mustowfi’s **present** (1940s composition), the lands **belonged to Dr. Stemp** — Mustowfi’s spelling of **[Étienne Stump](../../../people/etienne-stump.md)**.

Mustowfi calls him a **dentist**, not an innkeeper. The **Mahmoudieh guesthouse** run by **[Vera Obolensky](../../../people/vera-obolensky.md)** in the 1920s–1950s rests on Georges Obolensky’s memoir alone; this page documents **land ownership and Qajar legend**, not lodging.

## Spelling and nationality

- **استمپ (Stemp)** — Mustowfi’s form; the Mazda translation follows it.
- Mustowfi elsewhere calls Stump **“German”** in a dental anecdote; the vault treats **[Étienne Stump](../../../people/etienne-stump.md)** as **Swiss** (parish Tallinn, Beytoote/Najm-Abadi). The **ownership** claim does not depend on resolving that label.
