# *The Portfolio* (1836) — Charles Burgess, *Brief Notice* to T.H. Villiers

## Summary

Early **public reprint** of Charles Henry Burgess's memorandum on direct British trade to northern Persia via Trebizond, published in ***The Portfolio; or A collection of state papers, etc. illustrative of the history of our times*** (Google scan from Oxford, dated **1836** on Internet Archive). The piece appears under the heading **"THE LATE T. H. VILLIERS, ESQ. BY C. H. BURGESS."**

This is the **same text** as the 1832 memorandum and the 1983 *Iranian Studies* reprint already filed under [`../brief-notice-trade-northern-provinces-persia-villiers/`](../brief-notice-trade-northern-provinces-persia-villiers/). Villiers died in 1832 before the intended Parliamentary Paper appeared; a few copies were printed privately in **1833**; *The Portfolio* brought the memorandum into a wider documentary collection within a few years.
