# Quaderni del CRS Rovigno no. 24 — Zara industry

Passage listing Zara's interwar industries (PDF page 144):

> … thus "the cigarette industry… with the Manifattura Tabacchi Orientali and the Manifattura Zaratina Sigarette"; then "**Antonio Zerauschek**'s chocolate factory"; "a fishing-net factory, Sapri"; and "the Dalmata metallurgical industry, due to Emilian industrialists".

> There was also the "cereria" (wax works — giving the suburb its name "Ceraria"), a plant for wax production.
