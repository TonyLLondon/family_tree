# Quaderni del CRS Rovigno n. 24 — Antonio Zerauschek / Zara industry (2013)

## Summary

Academic note (*Quaderni del CRS Rovigno*, no. 24, 2013) summarising Zara's interwar industrial landscape. Names **Antonio Zerauschek** explicitly as owner of the chocolate factory, between the cigarette manufactories (MTO and Manifattura Zaratina) and the Sapri net factory.
