# Gazzetta Ufficiale — Bellini-Zerauschek cognome reduction (1931)

## Summary

Prefectural decree (**Zara**, 12 January 1931; published *Gazzetta Ufficiale* **30 January 1931**, n. 24): **Bellini-Zerauschek Giuseppe fu Giovanni** obtains Italianised surname **Bellini-Bressi**; same for children **Antonio**, **Carlo** (b. Zara 16 Oct 1906), **Mario**, **Oscar**, **Guido** (mother **Petkovich Luigia**). Collateral Zara line — **not yet linked** to Antonio Zerauschek (1889) in the tree.
