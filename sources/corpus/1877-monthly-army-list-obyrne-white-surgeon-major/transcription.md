## Notes

This is the **primary facsimile** for promotion to **Surgeon-Major** on **31 March 1877**, matching the Army Medical Department half-pay register and [William O'Byrne White](../../../people/william-obyrne-white.md) rank table.

The preceding **March/April 1877** issue (`monthly-army-list-1877-mar`) still shows his **1865 commission** date (`31 Mar. 65 Bengal`); the promotion first appears in the **May/June** list.

Station **Bengal** at promotion aligns with regimental posting in India (2nd Bn, 1st Foot at Jubbulpore in the Central Provinces). Son [Gerald Sebastian White](../../../people/gerald-sebastian-white.md) was born at Jubbulpore **12 February 1878**, ten months after this promotion.
