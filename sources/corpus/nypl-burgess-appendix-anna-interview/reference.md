# NYPL Burgess Persian Letters — Appendix: Anna Burgess interview (1880)

## Summary

Cornerstone **first-hand** testimony from **Anna Burgess (née Saginian)**, Leicester, **March 1880** — published as the appendix to Benjamin Schwartz's edition of the *Burgess Persian Letters* (*NYPL Bulletin*, 1942). Names **Daoud Khan Seguinoff** as father, **Tamar** as youngest sister (widow of Dr Cormick), family structure, Edward's death and Tabriz burial, and proximity of Daoud Khan's grave to Edward's.
