# *Missionary Herald* — David Khan: “I am their shepherd” (1845)

**Justin Perkins**, American Board missionary at **Oroomiah**, records a call on **David Khan, the Governor of the Nestorians**, in his journal for **June–July 1845**, printed in ***The Missionary Herald*** (Boston), Vol. **XLI**, No. **12** (**December 1845**), section **Nestorians: Journal of Mr. Perkins**, pp. **407–409**.

This is the vault’s **primary quotation** of Daoud Khan’s own words on the Nestorian mandate — pastoral metaphor, Muslim opposition, and Russian/English embassy support — distinct from [Rufus Anderson](../sources/rufus-anderson-1872-history-missions-oriental-churches.md)’s institutional summary of the **May 1845** appointment.
