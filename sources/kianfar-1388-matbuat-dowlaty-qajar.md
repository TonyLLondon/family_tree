# Kianfar — Qajar official press (1388)

**Jamshid Kianfar**, *Matbūʿāt-e dowlati-ye Īrān dar ʿaṣr-e Qājār*, *Payām-e Baharestān* (Spring 1388). Free PDF on ensani.ir.

Names **Edward Burgess (ادوارد برجیس / برجیس صاحب)** as *Vaqāye ʿ-e Ettefāqiye* translator, Mirza Jabbar as manager, and weekly pay to Burgess for foreign-news translation.
