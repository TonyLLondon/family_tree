# Source — *Rūznāma-ye Vaqāyeʿ-e Ettefāqiye* (NLIR facsimile, 1373)

Official lithograph facsimile of the Qajar **Tehran Gazette**, prepared by the **National Library of Iran** (سازمان اسناد و کتابخانه ملی), imprint **1373** (~1994). This is the **best complete hold** in the vault: four PDF volumes (**3,243** facsimile pages) under `media/docs/internet-archive/tehran-gazette/`.
