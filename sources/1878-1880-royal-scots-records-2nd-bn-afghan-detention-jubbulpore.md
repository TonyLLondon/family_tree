# Regimental Records of the Royal Scots — 2nd Bn detained at Jubbulpore (Afghan War 1878–1880)

**2nd Battalion, 1st Foot (Royal Scots)** garrisoned **Jubbulpore** from **1 March 1877**; twice ordered home but **detained in India** (Oct 1878, Dec 1879) owing to the **Second Anglo-Afghan War**; embarked for England **November 1880**. Battalion did not deploy to the frontier.
